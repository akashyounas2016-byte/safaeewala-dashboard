## Found and fixed today

1. **Villa 87b St 63d — three screens, three numbers.** A Pending Received of AED 540 (1 Oct) was entered while the client owed nothing, so no screen counted it. The client card showed Received = Billed − Due (AED 60), the profile AED 100. Now: money collected with nothing pending is the client's advance; card and profile show what was really paid (Received AED 640, Advance AED 580). The Pending Received popup now warns before such an entry. (R-FIN-12)
2. **Payments → Advance Balance and Client credit balances** showed only the opening advance — now the same formula as the Client Profile (AED 590 held by 2 clients). Adjustments, opening balances and active contracts added. (R-FIN-15)
3. **Jobs never kept their client.** `client_id` was missing from the list of fields sent to the database: 0 of 491 rows had it. That is how "Warehouse no.7 - Art corner Showroom" became a second profile after a rename. Fixed for every new / edited row. (R-DATA-14)
4. **Monthly client remarks** (e.g. Deep Cleaning → Sofa Cleaning) did not reach the coming visits — now they do, from today on. (R-SCHED-26)
5. Smaller: payment "Collected" now includes bookings without a sheet row (matches the history list); collection rate no longer mixes contract money into jobs.

## Recommendations

**Data & sync**
1. Link every job to its client by id (approve the one-time link). After that, client totals stop depending on the address text.
2. Make the client's rate mandatory for Bi-Weekly clients, so no visit is created at AED 0.
3. One money summary function per client (billed, received, pending, advance, adjustments) used by every screen — today the card, profile and Payments each add up on their own; today's fixes make them agree, a single function keeps them agreeing.

**Payments screen**
4. Date filter on the cards (this month / last month / custom) like the Job Sheet.
5. "Clients with both pending and advance" list — settle one against the other with one click.
6. Ageing view for dues (0–30 / 31–60 / 60+ days) with WhatsApp reminder per client (the helper exists).

**Operations**
7. Contract renewal: from the new contract-end alert, one click to extend the contract by 1 / 3 / 6 / 12 months.
8. Weekly report to the owner by WhatsApp / email: jobs, collected, pending, new leads, clicks.
9. Cleaner app check-in / check-out photo on each job (the check-in table exists) to prove the visit.

**Quality**
10. A short monthly "data health" run (this audit as a button in Settings → Data health) so mismatches show up the same day.
